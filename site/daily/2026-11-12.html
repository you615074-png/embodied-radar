Watch Your Step: Foothold Prediction for Humanoid Locomotion

RepoHumanoids & leggedleggedrobotics/foothold-prediction-model

What it is. This is the training and evaluation code for Watch Your Step: Anticipated Perception for Humanoid Locomotion by Matthias Heyrman, Chenhao Li, Ioannis Dadiotis, and Marco Hutter at ETH Zurich's Robotic Systems Lab and ETH AI Center. The system trains a Unitree G1 humanoid in Isaac Lab with two parts learned together: a foothold prediction model (FPM) that guesses the next two footholds from proprioception and the velocity command, and a locomotion policy that reads the elevation map only in 7x7 patches around those predicted footholds.

Why it matters. Most perceptive locomotion policies look at a wide swath of terrain every step, which costs compute and can be noisy. This work asks the robot to look where it is about to step instead. The policy transfers zero-shot to the real robot, per the project page.

Can you try it without a robot? The repo needs Isaac Sim 5.1, Isaac Lab 2.3.0, and rsl-rl-lib 3.3.0, so it won't run on a typical Mac. The project page links a video and a version of the policy that runs in the browser, though the repo itself does not include a web demo, so check the project page for what is actually live.
