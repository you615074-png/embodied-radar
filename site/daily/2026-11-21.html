SE3 Labs open-sources 500 bimanual teleop demos in LeRobot format

DatasetDatasetsse3labs/real-robodojo-teleop-500

SE3 Labs has published real-robodojo-teleop-500 on Hugging Face: 500 successful teleoperation episodes (about 6.3 hours) covering 10 real-world bimanual manipulation tasks from their RoboDojo benchmark, 50 episodes per task. Tasks range from packing objects into a box and hanging mugs to plugging in a charger and inserting tubes.

Why it matters: bimanual data from real hardware is still scarce and expensive to collect, and this set follows the LeRobot dataset format (v3.0), so it loads with the standard tooling instead of a custom loader. Everything is recorded on one station — two 6-DoF YAM arms with parallel grippers and three RealSense D405 stereo cameras (top-down, left wrist, right wrist) — at 30 fps, with 14-dim state/action vectors (12 joints + 2 grippers). Full-rate ~500 Hz arm logs are included as raw parquet files, and camera intrinsics live in meta/cameras.json. It is licensed CC-BY-4.0.

Can you try it without a robot? Yes, at least partially. The dataset page links LeRobot's browser visualizer, so you can scrub episodes on any machine. On a Mac you can pull the parquet metadata and train a small ACT or Diffusion Policy on a few episodes to practice the imitation-learning loop — you just can't evaluate on hardware.

Caveats: there is no accompanying paper, the README lists the homepage and paper as "More Information Needed," and all episodes are successes, so any policy evaluation will look rosier than reality.

Try it without a robot: Browse episodes on LeRobot's visualizer Space, then train ACT/Diffusion Policy on a few tasks on your Mac.
